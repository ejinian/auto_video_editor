# Hand-read (Claude, 2026-10-09) — what the 97 reels say

**Corpus:** 7 TikTok accounts, 97 reels, view counts public (no login, read through the
browser pane at human speed: profile → Popular / Latest tabs). Direct comps: @iansbrash
(3.4K followers, median 37.6K), @ayxanium (3.7K, median 41.3K — "Shahbazov", whose
original sound is on day2_3 / day2_5). Small: @aisquads (419), @humzakhannn (5.2K).
Adjacent: @aimn.dev (2.2K, education), @wex.ai.labs (14K), @ih8chz (53.8K, CS-student
skits, format only). Medians are from the posts captured, so they are biased up for the
two 90-video accounts; multiples there are conservative.

**The classifier named 9 of 97.** That is expected: these are not spoken hooks, they are
captions on silent deadpan clips, two to eight words. `hookscore.py` rates every winner
here WEAK (25-50) for the same reason. For Young Vibas the caption IS the on-screen line,
so do not let the scorer veto a two-word callout; use it only to kill greetings and
preambles.

## Formulas that over-index in the top third

1. **AI is about to replace me, played straight.** "POV bro is about to get laid off by AI"
   (1.3M, 34.6x, 12 s), "I am one anthropic model away from being unemployed" (322K),
   "lock in twin your job boutta be replaced" (139K), "Every dev nowadays" (545K, 13.2x).
   Counter-example on the same account: "claude can do my jaube so why should i" (1.9K).
   The premise alone is not enough; the straight face and a specific model name are.
2. **The cost bit.** "im bouta spend 100k on their api keys" (535K, 14.2x, 7 s), "please do
   not run up my openai api bill" (413K, 11x, 5 s), "I should run away and delete the aws
   account" (101K). A number plus a vendor name, over a clip of someone typing.
3. **Two-word callout over the clip.** "Full stack developer" (529K, 7 s), "Family first"
   (4.4M, 7 s), "My last hope" (1.0M), "We are Ken" (3.3M, 10 s), "What came after import"
   (1.1M). The video is the joke; the caption only names it.
4. **The obsession confession (tool promo).** "i am so obsessed with replit it shouldn't be
   allowed" (1.2M, 441x), "The claude method is insane" (593K, 218x), the newly.app posts.
   Giant multiples on tiny accounts — these read as sponsored or boosted. Note the
   shape, don't chase the numbers.
5. **Job-hunt pain, longer skits (37-62 s).** "ghosted after 8 rounds", "internal
   referral", "nobody lies harder than a swe on their resume" (95K-352K, 8-9x). Works at
   length only because it is a sketch with a turn; not our format yet.

## Structure of the top third vs the bottom third

- **Length:** outliers cluster at 5-13 s. iansbrash's five biggest are 5, 7, 7, 12 and 62 s;
  ih8chz's top ten are all 5-13 s. Our 10-18 s cuts fit; the 30 s default does not.
- **Caption length:** winners are 2-9 words, lowercase, no emoji, no ask. Bottom third
  on the same accounts: "Want me to drop tutorial?", "Save this for later", "Scroll to
  discover with replit!!!!" — asks, instructions, exclamation marks.
- **Sound:** "original sound" dominates the top third on the direct comps (the clip's own
  audio or a meme audio, not a licensed track).
- **First frame:** a person mid-action with a laptop in an incongruous place (gym, kitchen,
  bed) — our exact bit. Nobody in the corpus does the location escalation as a series.
