# patterns — what the niche's captions actually look like (read 2026-10-21)

Corpus: 3,770 posts, 3,498 TikTok. The slice that matters for Young Vibas is **997 TikTok
captions that are short (≤16 words), not selling anything, and about code / AI / Claude /
dev life** (`style=meme`, `niche=true`). 419 of those come from creators we have ≥5 posts
of, so they carry a `multiple` = views ÷ the creator's median. Everything below is from that
slice unless it says otherwise.

## 1. The register (numbers)

- **Median 7 words** before hashtags. A quarter are ≤4 words. Long captions are the guru
  accounts' thing.
- **Lowercase-only: 21%.** The niche mostly writes Sentence case; Ernest's all-lowercase is a
  voice choice, not the norm — fine, keep it, it reads deadpan.
- **Emoji: 25%** of captions, almost always ONE, used as punctuation at the end (💔 😭 🥀 🤔 🗿
  💀). Ernest's rule is none; the deadpan survives without them.
- **Hashtags: median 5**, stuck on the end, generic: #coding #ai #programming
  #softwareengineer #computerscience #claude #vibecoding #tech #codingmemes #startup #techbro.
  The caption text itself never contains the hashtag words.
- Punctuation is loose: no period, or one; commas dropped ("Family first, hahaha").
- Spelling is phone-spelling: "bouta", "boutta", "wdym", "allat", "larpers", "goated".

| **… lol / lmao / hahaha** (a tail that says "I know") | 6 | 11x | "Family first, hahaha" (4.4M, 60x) · "What came after import hahaha" (1.1M, 15x) · "claude absolutely grills me in his chain of thought lmao" (164k, 13x) |
| plain statement, no format | most | ~1-2x | "Full stack developer" (530k, 43x) · "The claude method is insane" (599k, 217x) · "We are Ken" (3.4M, 113x) |
| **a question to the audience** | 80 | **1.1x** | questions do NOT beat the account. "Would you have gotten caught?" (27k) is the best of a weak bunch |

Read: the winners are **a universal feeling with ONE tech noun in it**, in first person or
"bro / every dev" third person, stated flat. Asking and advising lose.

## 4. What Ernest's videos are, mapped onto this

The footage is "coding in an absurd place, deadpan". The corpus says the caption should NOT
describe the place (the video does that) and should NOT explain the joke. It should be the
line the guy in the video would say, flat, with one Claude/AI/dev word:

- the replaced-by-AI bit: "ai took over the coding so i took up tennis", "claude's running in
  the background. so am i."
- the absurd-confidence bit: "i don't code anymore. i press enter on beat.", "the techno is
  for me. the thinking is for claude."
- the coworker bit: "claude said "you're absolutely right" so i left", "claude said done. it
  was not done."
- the real-numbers bit: "$200 a month and i still have to hold the laptop", "we still have 2
  customers"
- the universal-excuse bit (remote work): "slack says active", "camera off on the standup.
  this is why.", "boss said "i don't care where you work from""

Formats to reach for first: **POV**, **imagine**, **i am / im**, **every dev**, a flat
statement with a **lol / hahaha** tail. Formats to avoid: a question to the viewer, "you"
advice, anything with "comment X" / "link in bio" / "tutorial".
